University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples (MIS)
Raimundo 118 classical guitar, performed by Brian Penkrot, December 11, 2011.
Recorded by Shane Hoose and Zach Zubow, University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios.
Project director: Lawrence Fritts.

Usage permission: https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html
Recording details: https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISguitar.html

The project's published permission states that its recordings may be downloaded
and used for any projects, without restrictions. This is the Iowa MIS permission,
not a claim that the source was published under Creative Commons CC0.

Prepared for Autostrum: isolated notes, tuning correction, low-frequency rumble
removal, mono conversion, peak leveling, short fades, and MP3 compression.
Preparation script and source download/hash: decisions/guitar-samples.md.
These conversions do not impose additional restrictions on the recordings.
